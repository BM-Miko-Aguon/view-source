The internet should be a free and open source community, constantly sharing information and new ways to improve each other's works. The more free, the more accessible information is, the more that knowledge can be used to improve day-to-day lives or to further understand each other and our works. The more tools that are given out for free to improve works easily, the more people are willing to create and work towards something greater for themselves or others.
